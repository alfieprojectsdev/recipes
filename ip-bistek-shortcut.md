### **IP Bistek (The No-Marinade Shortcut)**

*Yield: 5 Servings | Prep time: 5 mins | Cook time: 15 mins + pressurization*

---

## ingredients

* 800g - 1kg Beef breakfast steaks (thin cut)
* 3 large White onions (sliced into rings)
* 2 tbsp Cooking oil

**The "Dump and Go" Liquid Base** — whisk together in a measuring cup before you start:

* 1/2 cup Sprite
* 1/2 cup Soy sauce
* 1/4 cup Calamansi extract
* 2 tbsp White vinegar
* 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
* 1 tsp Black pepper

## instructions

1. **Saute the aromatics and beef (3 mins).** Set IP to **Saute - High**. Add the oil. Toss in half the sliced onions and stir 1 min until they just begin to soften; remove and set aside. Add the breakfast steaks and stir constantly for 1-2 mins, just to brown the outside slightly.
2. **Deglaze and mellow the vinegar (2 mins).** Pour the liquid base over the beef. Scrape the bottom of the pot with a wooden spoon to release any stuck bits. Bring to a hard boil and let it bubble **without stirring for 2 mins** to cook off the raw vinegar bite.
3. **Pressure cook (5 mins + pressurization).** Press **Cancel**. Lock the lid, valve to **Sealing**, then **Manual / Pressure Cook on High for 5 mins**.
4. **Quick release and finish (2 mins).** **Quick Release** as soon as the timer beeps. Once the pin drops, open the lid, press **Cancel**, then select **Slow Cook / Keep Warm**. Toss in the remaining raw onion rings plus the sauteed onions set aside earlier. Close the lid for 1-2 mins — residual heat softens the raw onions while keeping a slight crunch.
5. Serve immediately, straight from the pot.

## additional notes / alternative ingredients

* **Why no marinade:** The 30-min pre-marination in `pinoy-bistek.md` can be skipped entirely. High pressure forces the hybrid liquid into the thin breakfast steaks, acting as a rapid marinade while it cooks.
* **Sprite:** Stands in for sugar — it tenderizes and balances the soy/calamansi/vinegar acidity. Substitute 1/2 cup water + 1 tbsp brown sugar if none on hand.
* **Quick Release is deliberate here.** It breaks the usual "natural release for meats" rule, but thin breakfast steaks don't need the extra carryover, and QR is what keeps the total time down.
* **Related:** `pinoy-bistek.md` for the classic stovetop version.
