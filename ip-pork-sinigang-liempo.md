### **IP Pork Sinigang (Liempo, Make-Ahead Split)**

*Yield: 5 Servings | Prep time: 15 mins | Cook time: 35 mins + pressurization | Split across two sessions*

---

## ingredients

**The base**

* 1 kg Pork belly (liempo), cut into 1.5-2 inch chunks
* 1 medium Onion (quartered)
* 2 medium Tomatoes (quartered)
* 1 packet (40g) Tamarind soup base (sinigang mix) — adjust to taste
* 1-2 tbsp Fish sauce (patis)
* 6-7 cups Water

**Hardy vegetables**

* 1 medium Eggplant (talong), sliced diagonally into thick pieces
* 1 bunch String beans (sitaw), cut into 2-inch lengths
* 8-10 pieces Okra, tops trimmed

**Leafy greens (added at serving)**

* 1 large bunch Kangkong — leaves picked, tender stalks cut into 2-inch pieces

## instructions

### Part 1 — Night before

1. **Saute (optional but recommended).** Set IP to **Saute**. Sear the liempo chunks a few minutes until lightly browned. Add onions and tomatoes, cook until slightly softened. Splash in the patis.
2. **Pressure cook (35 mins + pressurization).** Pour in the water and scrape up the browned bits. Press **Cancel**. Lock the lid, valve to **Sealing**, then **Manual / Pressure Cook on High for 35 mins**.
3. **Release.** **Natural Release for 10 mins**, then manually release the remaining pressure.
4. **Stop here if holding overnight** — see *Hold options* below. Otherwise continue to step 5.
5. **Hardy veggies (3-5 mins).** Stir in the sinigang mix until dissolved. Drop in the eggplant, string beans, and okra. **Saute** 3-5 mins, just to tender-crisp.

### Part 2 — Morning / serving

6. **Reheat.** Return the inner pot to the IP and use **Saute** to bring to a rapid simmer, or transfer to a regular pot and boil on the induction hob.
7. **Wilt the kangkong.** Turn the heat **off entirely**. Submerge the leaves and tender stalks, cover, and leave **exactly 2 mins**. Residual heat cooks them through without turning them mushy or dulling the green.

## additional notes / alternative ingredients

* **Hold options (which one to pick at step 4):**
  * **Fridge (best texture):** finish step 5, cool to room temperature, refrigerate overnight. Reheat at step 6.
  * **Keep Warm (best convenience):** hold **only the pork and broth** — skip step 5 entirely. In the morning, **Saute** to a boil, then add the sinigang mix, eggplant, string beans, and okra for 3-5 mins before the kangkong. Vegetables stay crisp because they were never held.
* **Do not hold the finished soup on Keep Warm.** 8-10 hours in hot liquid disintegrates the eggplant, okra, and string beans into mush and thickens the broth; the liempo over-extracts, going stringy with a greasy layer on top.
* **10-hour shutoff.** Most IPs cut Keep Warm after 10 hours. If it shuts off before you are awake, the pot drops into the danger zone — plan to be up before then, or use the fridge.
* **Sourness:** start with most of the 40g packet, taste after the veggies go in, and add the rest only if needed. Cooked-down tomatoes already contribute acid.
* **Serve with:** extra patis and sliced siling labuyo on the side for dipping.
