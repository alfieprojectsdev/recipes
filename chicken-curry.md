### **Creamy Laksa-Style Chicken Curry (Induction)**

*Serves 5 | Prep time: 10 mins | Cook time: ~50 mins*

---

### **Ingredients**

* **Chicken:** 1kg, cut into serving pieces (bone-in is fine).
* **The Veggies:** 3 medium potatoes (quartered) and 1 large carrot (cut into chunks).
* **The Base:** 3 tbsp laksa paste + 1 tbsp curry powder (optional, whatever's left). *Start with 2 tbsp paste if it's a hot brand or the kids are eating.*
* **The Cream:** 250ml coconut cream + 250ml all-purpose cream.
* **Aromatics:** 1 onion (sliced) and 6 cloves garlic (minced). *Optional: a thumb of ginger with the garlic.*
* **Liquid:** 1 cup water. *If your coconut cream is a 400ml can, use all of it and cut the water to ½ cup.*
* **Seasoning:** 1 tbsp patis (to taste, add only at the end) and 1 tsp sugar.
* **Oil:** 3 tbsp.

---

### **Cooking Instructions**

1. **Fry the Veggies (medium-high, 1600W–2000W):** Heat oil in the pot. Fry the potatoes and carrots until the edges are lightly golden, about 5 mins. Set them aside so they don't go mushy later.
2. **Aromatics (medium, ~1000W):** In the same oil, sauté the garlic until fragrant, then add the onion and cook until soft. Induction heats fast, so stir often.
3. **Fry the Paste (medium, ~1000W):** Add the laksa paste and curry powder. Stir constantly for 2–3 mins until the paste darkens slightly and the oil turns red. Add a splash of water if it sticks.
4. **Sear the Chicken (medium-high):** Add the chicken and toss until coated and no longer pink outside, about 5 mins.
5. **Simmer (400W–600W):** Pour in the coconut cream and water. Bring to a boil, then drop to a simmer. Cover and cook for about 20 mins, until the chicken is tender.
6. **Veggies Back In (400W–600W):** Return the potatoes and carrots. Simmer uncovered for about 10 mins, until they're fork-tender and the sauce has thickened.
7. **Finish (lowest setting):** Stir in the all-purpose cream and sugar. Heat through for 2–3 mins without boiling, or it may split. **Taste first**, then add patis only if it needs salt. Laksa paste is already salty.

---

### **Notes**

* **Broth cube:** You don't need one, since the laksa paste and patis already give salt and depth. If you use one, dissolve ½–1 cube in the water at Step 5 and **skip the patis**. It helps more when you use mostly curry powder and little laksa paste.
* **Shellfish:** Laksa paste usually contains shrimp paste.
* **Scaling to 6:** Use 1.2kg chicken and add 1 more potato. The sauce stretches fine.
* **Induction:** If the paste or cream starts to catch, lift the pot off. The heat stops right away.
