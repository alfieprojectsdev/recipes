### **Munggo with Pata & Kalabasa (Stovetop Pressure Cooker)**

*Yield: 5 Servings | Prep time: 10 mins | Cook time: ~1 hr 20 mins*

---

## ingredients

* 1.5 cups Dried mongo (mung beans), rinsed
* 0.75 kg Pork pata (hock), chopped — approx. 3.5-4 cups
* 3 cups Kalabasa (squash), cubed
* 1.5 - 2 medium Tomatoes, chopped (0.75 cup)
* 1 large Red onion, chopped (0.75 cup)
* 6 cloves Garlic, minced
* 8 cups Water
* 3 Chicken broth cubes
* 2 tbsp Cooking oil
* 2 tbsp Fish sauce (patis), to taste
* 3 cups Spinach or malunggay, packed *(optional)*

## instructions

| # | Action | Time | Temp |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | **Saute aromatics.** Heat oil. Saute onions, garlic, and tomatoes until soft. | 5 mins | 160-180°C |
| 2 | **Brown the pata.** Add pata and fish sauce. Stir until the meat lightly browns. | 3 mins | 180°C |
| 3 | **Bring to pressure.** Add mongo, water, and broth cubes. Seal lid. | ~10 mins | 160°C |
| 4 | **Maintain pressure.** Once the valve rocks, drop heat to hold it steady. | 35 mins | 100°C |
| 5 | **Depressurize.** Turn off heat. Natural pressure release (NPR). | 15 mins | Off |
| 6 | **Simmer squash.** Open lid safely. Add kalabasa; simmer until tender. | 8 mins | 100°C |
| 7 | **Final touch.** Turn off heat. Stir in greens and let wilt. | 2 mins | Residual |

## additional notes / alternative ingredients

* **Not the Instant Pot version.** This is the stovetop pressure cooker on induction — see `instant-pot-munggo.md` for the IP version (no pata, no squash, 30 mins Manual High).
* **Temps map to induction settings**, not arbitrary numbers. Step 4's 100°C is the lowest setting that still holds a rocking valve; if yours stops rocking, nudge up one step rather than riding at 120°C.
* **Kalabasa goes in after depressurizing** — under pressure it disintegrates into the broth. If you *want* a thicker, sweeter sauce, add half at step 3 and half at step 6.
* **Scaling:** already scaled 1.5x for 5 people. The 8 cups of water assumes NPR; a quick release loses less liquid, so start at 7 cups if you ever cut the release short.
